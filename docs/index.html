Rallymix

Run the club's Americano night from one phone. Type the names, pick your courts and a format: Rallymix draws every round fairly, takes the scores and keeps a live table you can share with the group chat.

Formats

Americano

Super 8, Super 10, social mixer

A new partner every round. Every point you win goes on your own score.

Mexicano

The table sets the courts

After round one, the top four play together, the next four on the next court, so games stay close.

Mixed

Mixed Americano

Two groups, one from each in every team, partners rotate.

Teams

Team Americano / round robin

Fixed pairs or singles play every other team once.

King

King of the Court

Winners move up a court, losers move down, partners split.

Fair, even with odd numbers

Everyone gets the same number of matches (or as close as possible), rests rotate and nobody sits out twice in a row when it can be avoided. Partners repeat only once everyone has partnered everyone. If someone leaves or arrives late, only the rounds that haven't started are re-planned.

Help & support

How many rounds should we play?
Rallymix suggests a number where everyone plays the same number of matches. With 8 players on 2 courts, 7 rounds means everyone partners everyone exactly once.
Someone has to leave early.
Open the session, go to Players and switch them off. Rounds that haven't started are re-planned and their results so far still count. Late arrivals are added on the same screen.
What does sitting out earn?
By default nothing, but you can give half the points of a match or the round's average in Session settings, or rank by points per match.
Can I share the schedule?
Yes: the share button sends the current round or the table as a picture and text to any chat app.
What's in Rallymix Pro?
Every format is free for up to 8 players on 2 courts. Pro adds up to 48 players and 12 courts, player stats across sessions and CSV export. Restore purchases from Settings.
Where is my data?
Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. Deleting the app deletes your sessions.
