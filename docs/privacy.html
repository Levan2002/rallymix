Rallymix

Privacy Policy

Updated 1 October 2026

In short: Rallymix has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your sessions, players and scores stay on your iPhone.

Sessions, players and scores

The names you type, the rounds, scores and player list are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. If you use iCloud Backup they are part of your own encrypted device backup. The developer has no server and cannot see this data.

Sharing

When you share a round, the table or a CSV file, the app hands it to the iOS share sheet and you choose where it goes. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you share it.

Notifications

The round timer uses a local notification scheduled on your iPhone. No push service is used.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. The app uses RevenueCat to confirm your Pro status. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (which product, when and its status) and a random anonymous app user ID generated on your device. This data is used only to provide purchases and restores. It is not linked to your identity, not used for tracking and not sold.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a session from its menu, delete players in the Players list, or delete the app to remove everything. To ask about purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact the developer.
